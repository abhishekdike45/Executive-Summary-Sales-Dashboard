# 📊 Executive Summary Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Executive Summary Sales Dashboard is an interactive Excel
dashboard designed to analyze sales performance and provide
meaningful business insights.

This dashboard helps users understand revenue, profit, order
volume, product category performance, and country-wise sales
trends through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Track monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare revenue across different countries.
- Understand revenue contribution by sales channel.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Analyze sales data using interactive filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. KPI Summary
- Total Revenue: $645,223.18
- Total Profit: $296,384.25
- Gross Profit Margin: 46%
- Total Units Sold: 6,698
- Total Orders: 2,707
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): $348,838.93

### 2. Monthly Revenue & Profit
A line chart to compare monthly revenue and gross profit
and identify changes in sales performance.

### 3. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
A bar chart to compare revenue across countries and
identify important markets.

### 4. Revenue Share by Channel
A pie chart showing the percentage contribution of
different sales channels.

### 5. Region-Wise Comparison
A doughnut chart comparing sales performance across
different European regions.

### 6. Country-Wise Map Chart
A geographical visualization showing revenue distribution
across countries.

### 7. Interactive Filters
Slicers allow users to filter dashboard data by:
- Product Category
- Country
- Quarter

## 💡 Key Insights
